Optional settings: minimum stipend/salary, full-time vs internship, work authorization, graduation-year filters, and preferred company size/domain. Do not infer missing eligibility information.

### 5. Weekly outreach and content cadence

Suggested draft cadence: three LinkedIn posts per week; review one compact networking shortlist; recruiter outreach only for specifically relevant, source-verified opportunities. All sends/publications remain user-approved.

### 6. Resume source of truth

Confirm which resume PDF is the current base copy before tailoring applications. Keep the original immutable and store tailored variants separately.

### 7. External service permissions

When each integration is ready, the user chooses whether to connect it. Start read-only where possible. Do not request write scopes until the feature is tested and the user explicitly enables it.

## Decision log format

For every resolved item, record: decision, date, who confirmed it, evidence/source (if applicable), affected modules, and whether a historical record needs correction. Do not put secrets or unnecessary personal data in this file.
